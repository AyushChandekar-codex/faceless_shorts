# What Is The James Webb Telescope? — 2D short

A 44 s vertical (1080x1920, 30 fps) flat-2D explainer built with the **Inside Out** style in this repo:
`script.json` -> AI narration -> word-synced captions -> 16 procedurally drawn shots -> procedural music + SFX -> MP4.

- **`james-webb-explained.zip`** — the deliverable: the MP4, its cover frame and the publish copy (title, caption, hashtags, sources).
- **`james-webb-explained-cover.jpg`** — the cover/thumbnail frame.

Facts checked against NASA / jwst.nasa.gov ("About Webb") and JADES/UCSC + Space.com reporting:
launched 25 Dec 2021 to Sun-Earth L2 (~1.5 million km); 6.5 m primary of 18 gold-coated beryllium hexagons,
unfolded after launch; five-layer ~21x14 m sunshield; optics around 40 K (-233 C); not serviceable at L2;
galaxies confirmed at z~14, light from about 13.5 billion years ago.

Project sources live in the working tree (`jwst-2d/`: `script.json`, `src/shots.js`, `src/core/kit.js`).
